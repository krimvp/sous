sous

You call the order. The sous chef picks the cooks, mixes the ingredients and runs the line until the plate is served.

$ sous run "Add a --precision option to the CLI, with tests"

How the chef composes the kitchen

You stock the pantry and hire the cooks. For each part of the order the sous picks a cook, adds an ingredient and writes the recipe. Your goal is the order; each part becomes a plate.

A cooka harness: Claude Code, Codex or Pi
+
An ingredienta model from your pantry
+
A recipeinstructions: "you are an HR specialist…"
=
A stationa named agent, e.g. hr

You stock the kitchen

Hire your own cooks, and choose which models leads may use, per harness. Leads only cook with what is in the pantry; the cooks you name yourself bring their own.

Big orders get teams

A station can have its own sous, who splits its part again: as many levels as you allow with --max-depth.

Nothing leaves unchecked

Plates are merged one at a time and tasted on the merged result. If the kitchen stops, sous resume picks up mid-service.

A real service: order #0142

A calculator, built five levels deep on cheap Ollama models. Each pair of bars is one minute of the real run. The quiet stretch is one cook spending 8 m 44 s on the parser while the line waited.

The menu

To start

npm install -g @krimvp/sousNode 22.19+, git, and Claude Code, Codex or Pi

Mains

sous run "goal"let the sous plan it
sous run graph.jsona fixed menu of steps, gates and leads
--agents agents.jsonyour cooks and pantry, for goal runs
--max-depth 4a taller brigade (default 3)

During service

sous statusthe live brigade, and questions for you
sous answer <node> "…"answer, or steer a lead
sous approve <gate>pass a plate that needs your OK
sous cancel <node>86 it, and everything under it
sous resumeback on after a power cut

After

sous export atifthe whole service as a trace (or otlp to Langfuse)

Leads plan on your Pi login (Ollama Cloud or ChatGPT). Run sous inside the git repository the cooks should work in.
